Method

How it works

Corpus
ChunkingSentence-packed chunks of ~160 words with 30 words of overlap, each prefixed with drug, brand names and section.
RetrievalBM25 + bge-small embeddings fused with reciprocal rank fusion, filtered to the drug named in the question, with section hints. A cross-encoder re-ranker was tested and left out: it lowered top-1 accuracy.
GenerationQwen2.5 (4-bit, CPU) answers only from the top 4 passages and cites them. An entailment check removes sentences the passages don't support, and refuses when none are left.
