Method

How it works

Corpus
ChunkingSentence-packed chunks of ~160 words with 30 words of overlap, each prefixed with drug, brand names and section.
RetrievalBM25 + bge-small embeddings fused with reciprocal rank fusion, filtered to the drug named in the question, re-ranked by a cross-encoder.
GenerationQwen2.5-1.5B (4-bit, CPU) answers only from the top 4 passages, cites them, and refuses when they don't answer.
